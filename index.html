💡 Factor BINGO Rules & Math Guide

What is a Factor? A factor is a number that divides evenly into another number without leaving a remainder.

Example: Target = 36
Factors of 36 are: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36
(Because 36 ÷ 9 = 4 exactly!)

How to Play:

  • When a target number is called, inspect your 5x5 board.
  • Click any number on your board that is a **factor** of the called target number.
  • If correct, your tile turns green and updates your live dot grid across all opponents!
  • If you have no valid factors on your board, click "No Factors on Board (Pass)".
  • Get 5 claimed tiles in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to claim **BINGO!**
